## Why this matters

Scope creep rarely arrives as a villain speech. It shows up as "quick," "small," and "while you're in there."
If you bill projects or capped hours, every unclear yes is a silent discount.

This brief lists **12 warning signs** across sales, kickoff, and delivery -- plus a **three-day action plan**. It does not include a change-order template or the other paid Core files. Those stay on the Client Guard Kit page (Notion structure, 5 prompts, 3 SOPs).

---

## Red flags 1-4 -- Sales & fit

1. **No budget range, only "flexible."** -> Ask for a band before a custom proposal; offer 2 packaged tiers.
2. **Committee with no named decider.** -> Require one approval owner in writing.
3. **"We'll know it when we see it."** -> Refuse to start without a one-sentence definition of done.
4. **Rush + undefined scope.** -> Rush fee + narrower SOW, or decline.

## Red flags 5-8 -- Kickoff

5. **Deposit skipped "because we're friends / you'll invoice later."** -> No deposit, no start.
6. **Assets "coming soon" with a hard launch date.** -> Slip the end date in writing or pause.
7. **Feedback in 4 channels at once.** -> Pick one channel; ignore the rest.
8. **Unlimited revisions promised in the sales call.** -> Replace with a numeric cap in the SOW.

## Red flags 9-12 -- Delivery

9. **"Tweak" that needs new modules/pages.** -> Change order, not a revision.
10. **New stakeholder at week 3 rewriting goals.** -> Re-kickoff or kill fee conversation.
11. **Revision cap hit, still "just polish."** -> Boundary reply + paid path.
12. **Final files before final payment.** -> Hold source; send preview.

## Three-day action plan

**Day 1:** Write your default revision cap + deposit % on a sticky (or Notion).
**Day 2:** Add a Change Request database (or list) -- even a simple table.
**Day 3:** Save two scripts you write yourself: a change-order email and a revision-boundary reply. This free brief does not include those templates.
