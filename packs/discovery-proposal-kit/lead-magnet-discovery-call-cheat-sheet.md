## Before the call (15 minutes)

1. **Lead row** — name, company, source. Don’t invent budget.  
2. **Agenda** — problem, success definition, decision-maker, constraints, close path.  
3. **Hard nos** — no live free deliverables; no unlimited-revision promises.  
4. **Price floor** — decide privately; you’ll type numbers later.  
5. **Intention** — “I’ll send Good / Better / Best today.”

---

## On the call — 7 questions that matter

1. What breaks if nothing changes?  
2. What does done look like in one sentence?  
3. Who signs fee + scope?  
4. Any hard date?  
5. Budget band — even rough? (OK if unknown — don’t invent.)  
6. What’s already decided vs open?  
7. Why now?

**Close line:** “I’ll email three priced options today. When can you decide?”  
If undecided → **calendar next-step only**. No “only 2 slots.” No countdown.

---

## Same-day path (do not skip)

1. Dump raw notes  
2. Problem brief (their words)  
3. Good / Better / Best with **your** prices  
4. Proposal email with disclaimer: **process + copy — not a binding contract / not attorney work product**  
5. Log: Proposal sent

---

## Red flags (cut free consulting)

- “We’ll know it when we see it” without a success sentence  
- Committee, no named decider  
- DIY lean + “just brainstorm with us”  
- Rush + fuzzy scope  
- Asks for free mockups on the call
