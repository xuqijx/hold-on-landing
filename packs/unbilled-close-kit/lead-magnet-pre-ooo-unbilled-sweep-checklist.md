## Pre-OOO sweep checklist

1. **Window** — pick the unbilled date range before OOO.  
2. **Pull clues** — calendar blocks, Slack/Teams “quick” threads, “I’ll invoice later” notes.  
3. **Redact** — then extract candidates (kit prompt does the table).  
4. **Hours honesty** — only durations in the paste; missing → `unknown` (you confirm; never invent).  
5. **Goodwill vs billable** — skip relationship coffee; bill real asked work.  
6. **One final invoice** — line items + optional pre-OOO cover in a **single** email.  
7. **Send same day** if hours are Known/Confirmed.  
8. **Hard stop** — Sent = done here. Ignored later → Collections Chase Kit (not this sheet).
