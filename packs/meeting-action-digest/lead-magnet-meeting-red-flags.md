## Page: Why this matters

Meetings don’t fail in the Zoom window. They fail in the hour after — when notes stay messy, owners stay unnamed, and “we’ll circle back” becomes next week’s agenda.

This brief lists **12 red flags** across before / during / after — plus a **72-hour fix**. The full kit’s hero path is simple: **paste messy notes once → one digest with owner + due on every action.**

---

## Red flags 1–4 — Before the call

1. **No written goal for the meeting.** → One sentence in the invite: “Decide X / unblock Y.”  
2. **Decision-maker not invited (or unclear).** → Name the decider on the calendar description.  
3. **Same topic as last week with no Decision ID.** → Dig up what was “agreed” or admit it was never locked.  
4. **Agenda is a therapy dump.** → Cap to 3 bullets; park the rest.

## Red flags 5–8 — During

5. **Actions said out loud with no owner.** → Stop and ask “Who owns this?” before moving on.  
6. **“Soon / next week / ASAP” with no date.** → Force a calendar day.  
7. **New work sneaks in without parking.** → Parking lot now; sort later — don’t fake P0s.  
8. **Reopening last week’s call mid-standup.** → Point to the decision log; reopen only on rule.

## Red flags 9–12 — After

9. **Notes sit in a personal Doc nobody opens.** → Digest + Actions board within 10 minutes.  
10. **Slack “reminders” with no Due field.** → If it isn’t on the board with due, it isn’t real.  
11. **You invent owners because asking feels awkward.** → Leave UNKNOWN and ASK — inventing burns trust.  
12. **Blocked items with no blocker sentence.** → Name who/what; then nudge that person.

## Action checklist (print this)

- [ ] Meeting has a one-sentence goal  
- [ ] Decider named  
- [ ] Every action has **owner + due** (or UNKNOWN/ASK)  
- [ ] Decisions locked with a short log line  
- [ ] Parking lot sorted (defer / drop / new thread)  
- [ ] Meeting Status = Digested same day  
- [ ] Weekly sweep on calendar (This week due / Blocked / Needs decision)
