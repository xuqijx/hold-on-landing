## Why this matters

Late invoices rarely clear because you "thought about them." They clear because someone sent a clear ask on a clear day.

This brief gives you a **four-stage follow-up map**, **six invoice facts**, and **example opening lines** you can paste and finish. It stops at those opening lines. Full email sequences are not included. The paid Core kit is a separate product: Notion aging, six full prompts, and weekly SOPs, described on the Collections Chase Kit page.

## Six invoice facts (every chase)

1. Invoice number
2. Amount
3. Original due date
4. Days overdue (honest)
5. Pay link or method
6. One ask (pay / confirm paid / propose date)

Skip the essay. Skip the apology spiral.

---

## Four-stage follow-up map

| Stage | When | Tone | Goal |
|-------|------|------|------|
| D1 | ~1 day late | Warm nudge | Assume good intent |
| D7 | ~7 days | Polite + clear | Get a date or confirmation |
| D14 | ~14 days | Urgent + hold preview | Pay or open a plan |
| Final | ~21-30 days | Firm, factual | Deadline + consequences you will keep |

---

## Example opening lines (finish with your facts)

These are opening lines, not full emails.

**D1:** "Quick check -- INV-____ was due ____. If it's already in flight, send the receipt; otherwise here's the pay link..."

**D7:** "Following up on INV-____ ($____), now __ days past due. Can you share an expected payment date, or tell me if AP needs a resend/PO?"

**D14:** "INV-____ is __ days overdue. Per our agreement, [hold policy]. Pay link: ____. If cash-flow is tight, reply and I'll send a 2-pay plan."

**Final:** "Final notice on INV-____. Please pay or reply with a written plan by ____. If silent, [hold / pause / review path]."

## Three-day action plan

**Day 1:** List every open invoice with due date + amount (even a Notes app).
**Day 2:** Put the next chase date on each overdue row.
**Day 3:** Send one D-matched message using the lines above -- then stop rewriting at midnight.
