## 30-day checklist

1. **Effective date** — pick when the new rate bills (e.g. 2027-01-01).  
2. **Send-by** — aim ≥30 days prior (≈2026-12-02 for a Jan 1 effective).  
3. **Roster** — list ≥3 active clients (empty board is fine on day one).  
4. **Score** — Keep / Raise / Fire (default lean Raise if ≥12 months since last raise or clearly below market).  
5. **One plan** — old → new → effective date for your **first** Raise pick.  
6. **One email** — soft *or* direct notice in your voice.  
7. **Log send** — track reply: accept / scope / pause.  
8. **Hard stop** — when they acknowledge → bill at new rate. Overdue *old* invoices → Collections Chase Kit.

**Optional (not first-run):** rate-math worksheet — planning only; not an income promise.
