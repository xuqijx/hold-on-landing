Overdue invoice follow-up sequence

A late invoice usually needs a dated, factual note — not a longer apology. Copy the four emails below. They cover the due date, one week later, two weeks later, and day 30. The tone stays professional. Nothing here threatens a lawsuit, a collections agency, or a credit report.

How to use the four notes

Send one note on the matching day, then stop rewriting it at midnight. Fill the brackets with your real invoice. Replace the pay link with the method you already use. If your contract names a different cadence, follow the contract.

Before the first send, have these six facts ready: invoice number, amount, original due date, days overdue (honest; zero on the due date), pay link or method, and one ask (pay, confirm it was sent, or propose a date).

1. Due date — assume it is in their queue

Purpose. The invoice is due today. Remind them once, point at the pay link, and leave an easy way to say it is already scheduled. Do not apologize for invoicing.

Subject: Invoice [number] is due today

Hi [name],

Invoice [number] for [amount] is due today, [due date].
Pay here: [pay link]

If it is already scheduled, a receipt or expected send date is enough.
Otherwise the same link covers it.

Thanks,
[your name]

2. Day 7 — ask for a date, not a story

Purpose. A week has passed. Restate the facts and ask for one of two things: confirmation it was paid, or a payment date. Offer a resend in case accounts payable never saw it.

Subject: Following up on invoice [number] — 7 days past due

Hi [name],

Checking on invoice [number] for [amount], due [due date].
It is now 7 days past due.
Pay link: [pay link]

Could you share an expected payment date, or confirm it was already sent?
If AP needs the PDF or a PO again, reply and I will resend today.

Thanks,
[your name]

3. Day 14 — keep it factual, offer a short plan

Purpose. Two weeks late is no longer a missed inbox. Repeat the amount and the link. Ask them to pay or reply with a written date. A two-payment plan is an offer, not a penalty.

Subject: Invoice [number] is 14 days overdue

Hi [name],

Invoice [number] for [amount] was due [due date] and is now 14 days overdue.
Pay link: [pay link]

Please pay, or reply with the date you will send it.
If cash flow is tight, say so and I will reply with a two-payment schedule for this invoice only.

Thanks,
[your name]

4. Day 30 — one clear status check

Purpose. A month later, ask for payment or a dated written plan. Stay specific. Do not invent late fees, legal steps, or a hold unless those are already in the agreement you both have. If you have no written hold policy, leave that sentence out.

Subject: Status check on invoice [number] — 30 days past due

Hi [name],

This is a status check on invoice [number] for [amount], due [due date].
It is 30 days past due.
Pay link: [pay link]

Please pay or reply with a written plan (amount and date) by [reply-by date].
I will follow the plan you confirm in writing.

Thanks,
[your name]
